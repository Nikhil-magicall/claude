# AI SDR Platforms

> Similar to Artisan, 11x, UnifyGTM, and Flowworks — autonomous AI Sales Development Representatives for B2B outbound.

---

## 1. AiSDR
**Website:** aisdr.com  
**What it does:** Fully autonomous AI SDR handling prospecting, personalized email/SMS outreach, reply handling, and meeting booking. Known for transparent published pricing (starts at $900/mo) — unlike most competitors that gate pricing behind a sales call.  
**Best for:** SMB/mid-market teams wanting a no-contract entry into autonomous SDR.

---

## 2. Salesforge — Agent Frank
**Website:** salesforge.ai  
**What it does:** Email-focused AI SDR with two modes: full auto-pilot (Frank researches, writes, sends, and replies autonomously) or co-pilot (Frank drafts, human approves). Strong deliverability infrastructure built in.  
**Best for:** Teams that want to start in co-pilot mode and graduate to full autonomy.

---

## 3. Amplemarket — Duo
**Website:** amplemarket.com  
**What it does:** Deploys three specialized AI agents in concert — one for prospect research, one for copywriting, one for reply handling — with optional one-click human approval gates. More of an AI-augmented rep model than full replacement.  
**Best for:** Sales teams that want human oversight without killing throughput.

---

## 4. Piper by Qualified
**Website:** qualified.com  
**What it does:** Inbound-focused AI SDR that converts website visitors via live chat. Qualifies prospects in real time based on intent signals and CRM data, then books meetings on the spot. Pairs with Salesforce natively.  
**Best for:** High-traffic B2B SaaS companies with a strong inbound motion.

---

## 5. Warmly
**Website:** warmly.ai  
**What it does:** Identifies anonymous website visitors, enriches them with firmographic and contact data, and triggers automated outreach via email, LinkedIn, and chat at the moment of intent. More signal-orchestration layer than pure SDR.  
**Best for:** Teams that want to turn website traffic into pipeline without adding headcount.

---

## 6. Nooks
**Website:** nooks.ai  
**What it does:** AI parallel dialer combined with SDR automation. Known primarily for its AI-powered calling (parallel dialing, voicemail drop, live coaching) and has expanded into AI sequencing and prospecting workflows.  
**Best for:** Phone-heavy outbound teams and SDR managers wanting AI coaching + automation.

---

## 7. Regie.ai
**Website:** regie.ai  
**What it does:** Enterprise-grade AI platform for sales content generation, sequence automation, and SDR workflow orchestration. Integrates deeply with CRMs and SEPs (Outreach, Salesloft). Targets $30K+ budgets.  
**Best for:** Enterprise sales orgs standardizing messaging across large SDR teams.

---

## 8. Landbase
**Website:** landbase.com  
**What it does:** Deploys the GTM-1 Omni — an agentic AI action model that coordinates multiple specialized sub-agents for prospecting, research, personalization, and outreach across channels. Positions as a full go-to-market AI layer.  
**Best for:** Companies wanting a fully agentic, multi-channel outbound system.

---

## 9. Jason AI by Reply.io
**Website:** reply.io  
**What it does:** AI SDR agent inside the Reply.io platform that handles end-to-end outbound: lead research, multichannel sequence execution (email, LinkedIn, calls), personalization, reply classification, and meeting booking. Entry pricing starts at ~$59/user/mo with full AI SDR plans scaling to $3K+/mo.  
**Best for:** Teams already using Reply.io for sales engagement who want to layer in AI autonomy.

---

## 10. Coldreach
**Website:** coldreach.ai  
**What it does:** Intent-driven AI SDR that scrapes real-time signals from LinkedIn and the web, identifies buying intent, writes hyper-personalized messages based on that context, and automates full outreach sequences including smart follow-ups.  
**Best for:** Outbound teams that prioritize signal-based personalization over volume blasting.
