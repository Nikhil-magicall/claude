# Cold Email Sequence: US Recruitment & Staffing Agencies

**Sender:** InboxKit

**Audience:** Founders, managing partners, and heads of business development at US recruitment and staffing agencies (executive search, contingency, RPO, niche technical staffing).

**Angle:** Recruiting is one of the few industries where the people you cold email actually want to hear from you. Candidates want to hear about roles. Hiring managers want to hear about talent. One channel feeds both sides of the pipeline. InboxKit runs the channel and handles deliverability, so the agency only touches replies.

**Offer:** We run a live campaign for them so they see it work firsthand before committing.

**Proof points:** Magna Search Group and Zaelot run their entire pipeline through InboxKit, candidate side and client side, including executive search.

**Merge fields:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{niche}}` (e.g. "SaaS sales", "healthcare", "finance"), `{{sender_name}}`

**Cadence:** Day 1, Day 4, Day 8. Steps 2 and 3 reply in the same thread.
