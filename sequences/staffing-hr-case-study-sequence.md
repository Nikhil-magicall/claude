# InboxKit — Staffing & HR Firms
## 3-Step Sequence (audit-led)

**ICP:** Staffing agencies, executive search, RPO, and HR consultancies (5–200 employees)
**Best-fit trigger:** Already running Outreach, Salesloft, or HubSpot — and already paying cold callers
**Core insight:** They didn't fail at cold email. They sent everything from one domain and it never landed.
**Lead offer:** Free inbox placement audit (not a call)
**Paid offer:** 30-day parallel pilot, placement-guaranteed
**Named references:** Magna Search Group (magnasearchgroup.com), Zaelot (zaelot.com)
**Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 8. Emails 2 and 3 send as replies on the same thread.

> ⚠️ Before launch: confirm written OK from Magna Search Group and Zaelot to be named in cold outbound, check that "20 meetings a week" is accurate and attributed to the right firm, and fill every `[bracket]` with a real number. Ship no stat you can't back up on the call.

## Notes for the sender

**Why audit-first, not call-first:** this list has already been disappointed by cold email once, so "book a call" asks them to risk it twice. The audit proves your diagnosis in their own data instead of asserting it, it self-qualifies (anyone who hands over their sending domain has a live problem and budget), and you walk into the call already knowing their domain health — prescribing, not discovering.

**Guarantee placement, not meetings.** Meetings depend on their list, their copy, their vertical, and how fast their team follows up. You control none of that; you'd be underwriting their sales team. Inbox placement is yours, so promise that — a staffing owner who's been sold a meetings guarantee before will trust the narrower one more.

**Deliver the audit properly.** A screenshot of primary vs. promotions vs. spam across Gmail and Outlook seeds, one line on what's causing it, one line on what changes. It has to be forwardable to their partner without you in the room.

**Targeting:** prioritise firms with Outreach, Salesloft, or HubSpot in their tech stack. They've bought into outbound, budget exists, and they're the ones most likely to be disappointed with what it's returning. A firm with no sales stack needs a different, longer pitch.

**Personalization worth one line:**
- *"Saw [N] open roles on your site — that's a lot of sourcing volume for one domain."*
- Swap "agencies" for their vertical to match the list.

**Do not:**
- Claim a placement, revenue, or reply-rate number for a named client without their sign-off
- Let "20 meetings a week" go out unqualified if it's one firm's number, or a peak rather than a run rate — a recruiter will ask for the breakdown on the call
- Trash Outreach/Salesloft by name — they chose it, and the pitch is that we complement it
- Promise to replace their callers; the framing is that you make the callers' day worth more
- Send all three from the same inbox at volume — rotate across the pool
- Add images, tracking pixels, or link-heavy signatures to email 1

**Split-test worth running:** a client-BD version of email 1 aimed at owners and BD leads, where the pain is winning clients rather than sourcing candidates. Sourcing pain lands with delivery leads; client acquisition lands with whoever owns revenue.
